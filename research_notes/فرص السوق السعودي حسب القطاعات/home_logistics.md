# Saudi Market Gaps: Home & Housing, Logistics & Storage (as of Oct 2026)

Method note: about 17 tool calls (web search; direct page fetches of wamda.com and amlak.net.sa failed with DNS errors, so several facts rest on search-result summaries of those pages rather than full-page reads). Claims marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from self-published, aggregator or training-knowledge sources. Items already known to the coordinator (no Saudi P2P storage, furniture-rental subscription, junk-removal app, AI virtual staging, P2P parking; BorrowMe in Dubai; Gathern, Ejaro/Sharik exist) are not re-researched here.

## Q1. What are the biggest unsolved pains for Saudi renters, buyers, landlords and small sellers (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
Renters face fast-rising rents (enough to trigger a 5-year rent freeze in Riyadh from Sept 2025), large annual or semi-annual upfront payments, and opaque property condition. Buyers, a fast-growing group of first-time homeowners, lack standard pre-purchase inspection and design/fit-out guidance. Movers and small sellers depend on fragmented WhatsApp/Haraj providers for moving and bulky jobs, while parcel shipping for e-commerce sellers is already well served.

### Cited Findings
**Renting / landlords**
- From 25 Sept 2025, a 5-year freeze on rent increases covers residential and commercial leases inside Riyadh's urban boundaries, by Cabinet approval and royal decree. Officials cited rapid post-COVID rent rises. Violators can be fined up to 12 months' rent, and whistleblowers get a share of the fines. A vacant, previously leased unit is fixed at its last registered Ejar contract value. REGA may extend the freeze to other cities with CEDA approval — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-announces-rent-freeze-in-riyadh-for-five-years-1.500283302); [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2616697/amp); [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/saudi-arabia-freezes-riyadh-rents-five-years). (Fine details partly come from secondary summaries; check against SPA/REGA text.)
- Ejar (REGA's official lease-registration platform) had recorded over 10 million rental contracts by Sept 2024 — [REGA/Argaam coverage](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1537207). **[UNVERIFIED]** Ken Research puts 2025 volume at about 3.31M contracts, of which about 2.51M were residential — [Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-property-management-services-market) (vendor report, weak).
- Riyadh accounted for 31.4% of rental transactions in an earlier Rental Index (2022) — [Amlak](https://amlak.net.sa/en/35258/).
- The upfront lump-sum rent burden is big enough to support several funded "Rent Now Pay Later" startups (see Q2). This is indirect evidence that the pain is real and monetisable — [Wamda/Scenenoise on Rize](https://dev.scenenoise.com/Business/Proptech-Rize-Secures-35-Million-to-Expand-Across-Saudi-Arabia); [Ejari](https://dev.scenenoise.com/Business/Saudi-Proptech-Ejari-Raises-14-65-Million-to-Expand-RNPL-Services).

**Buying**
- Saudi household homeownership reached 65.4% at end-2024, ahead of the 2025 target of 65%, up from 47% in 2016. The 2030 target is 70% — [Amlak](https://amlak.net.sa/en/82829/); [Gulfbase](https://gulfbase.com/news/saudi-arabia-surpasses-2025-homeownership-target-a-year-early/210548). A later figure of 66.24% is reported only by TradeArabia — [TradeArabia](https://tradearabia.com/News/467182/Saudi-Arabia-homeownership-rises-to-66.24_Percent-exceeds-target) (single source).
- Over 48,000 Saudi families moved into their first home in H1 2025 through Sakani — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1832782). This is a steady flow of first-time buyers who need moving, furnishing, design and inspection.
- No search result showed a mandatory pre-purchase inspection for resale homes. REGA's field "inspections" target broker compliance (licensing, ads), not buildings — [REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/rega-conducts-18-joint-inspection-campaigns-in-may-to-ensure-compliance-in-real-estate-market). Brokers face fines up to SAR 200,000 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/world%2Fgulf%2Fsaudi%2Fsaudi-real-estate-brokers-face-fines-of-up-to-sr200000-for-violations-1.500661448).
- Buyers find pre-purchase villa inspections mostly through individual engineers on Haraj classifieds (covering cracks, plumbing, electrical, AC, water salinity), priced by area — [Haraj listing](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11182867036/Expert_Building_Inspector_in_RiyadhDetecting_Hidden_Defect/); [Haraj listing 2](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11181836478/Saudi_Engineer_for_Inspecting_Villas_Apartments_and_Building/). This shows demand served informally.

**Moving / bulky items**
- No official Transport General Authority platform for furniture moving was found. TGA-regulated app categories in results were ride-hailing and food/parcel delivery — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/saudia/fgup6kbzwv). Moving firms take quotes by phone or WhatsApp — [press release example](https://www.mydailyrecord.com/online_features/press_releases/10-year-riyadh-mover-offers-local-and-international-furniture-relocation/article_cb8492d0-28f0-5c25-a4fe-91dc0037b5dd.html) (weak, PR).
- One App Store app describing itself as "the first Saudi app for moving furniture between cities" has a 3.0 rating from 2 reviews, so traction is negligible — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1435730716).

**Small sellers**
- Shipping and fulfilment for e-commerce SMEs is served by several funded aggregators (Torod, OTO, Salasa; see Q2). The pain has shifted from "no access to couriers" to cost, COD and returns. **[UNVERIFIED]** This is an inference, not a cited stat.

### Inferences
- The rent freeze makes existing-tenant contracts more valuable, so tenants will move less and landlords will scrutinise new tenants and unit condition more. Documented move-in/move-out condition and deposit evidence also become more important when rent can't be raised to recover damage.
- The first-time homeowner flow (tens of thousands of families every half-year) creates repeat demand for inspection, fit-out, design and moving. Most of it is currently met via Haraj, WhatsApp and Instagram.

### Gaps
- No official 2025 Ejar full-year totals were found, and no renter demographic split (Saudi vs expat, income).
- No survey data was found on tenant–landlord disputes, deposit disputes, or maintenance complaints (the Ejar dispute/"Sanad" figures were not found).
- No hard data was found on the size of the Saudi moving or junk-removal market.

Rent financing, shipping aggregation and fulfilment are strongly contested by VC-backed Saudi players. Home maintenance and storage are moderately contested. Pre-purchase inspection has one specialist platform (Ayen). Moving marketplaces, interior e-design and rental condition-reporting are weak or empty.

### Inferences
- Avoid as a solo founder: RNPL/rent finance (capital-heavy, SAMA-regulated, three funded players), e-commerce shipping aggregation (Torod/OTO/Salasa), and operator-run storage (WheeKeep).
- Ayen shows that regulators (Saudi Council of Engineers) are building a formal inspection profession. That is a tailwind for adjacent digital tools, but a direct competitor for buyer inspections.

### Gaps
- MAGNiTT and Monsha'at databases were not accessible. Funding for Mangoul, Ayen and Nabeeh amounts is undisclosed.
- I couldn't verify whether Ejar itself offers a move-in condition-report feature (no evidence either way).
- Errand/courier apps (Mrsool, Jahez, SPL "Sobol") were not checked for bulky or C2C item coverage.

## Q3. Which proven Western models have no strong Saudi equivalent? (2–3 concrete gaps per sector, beyond the already-known list)

### Takeaway
The best fits for a design/digital founder with limited capital are asset-light software or marketplace plays. In housing: (1) a rental move-in/move-out condition report and deposit-evidence app, (2) flat-fee online interior e-design for first-time homeowners, and (3) a fair-rent/rent-freeze compliance checker. In logistics: (1) an on-demand "few items / single bulky item" moving app, (2) a vetted moving-quote marketplace with fixed prices, and (3) the known gaps (P2P storage, junk removal) bundled with the first two.

### Cited Findings & Gap Profiles

**HOME GAP 1 — Rental condition reports and deposit-evidence app (B2C/B2B SaaS for landlords, brokers and tenants)**
- Western model: UK "inventory/check-in report" apps (e.g., InventoryBase, Inventory Hive) and US move-in checklist apps; Spectora (US inspection-report software, which acquired HomeGauge in Apr 2025) — [Coverager](https://coverager.com/spectora-acquires-homegauge/). Spectora revenue estimates conflict ($5M vs $10M) and no funding data was found — [Bitscale](https://bitscale.ai/directory/spectora-home-inspection-software) [UNVERIFIED]. UK success evidence comes from training knowledge only: deposit protection rules make check-in inventories standard practice **[UNVERIFIED, no source fetched]**.
- Local problem: Under the Riyadh rent freeze, landlords can't raise rent to recover wear or damage, and vacant units are pegged to the last Ejar value. This makes documented unit condition and deposit disputes more important — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-announces-rent-freeze-in-riyadh-for-five-years-1.500283302).
- Demand evidence: about 2.5M residential leases per year **[UNVERIFIED Ken Research]**; over 10M Ejar contracts cumulative — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1537207).
- Competitors: Ayen (purchase inspection, engineer-led) — [StartupCentrum](https://startupcentrum.com/startup/ayen-platform-for-property-inspections-co). For rental check-in/check-out condition reporting: **none found**.
- Revenue model: per-report fee (landlord/broker), SaaS seats for brokerages and property managers, upsell to an engineer inspection via partners.
- Difficulty/capital: **Low** (mobile app with photo/video timestamps and PDF/Arabic reports). Main risk is adoption: it needs broker/property-manager channels.
- Regulatory notes: Engineering inspection opinions need Saudi Council of Engineers-accredited engineers ([Haraj listing claims SCE plus Ayen accreditation](https://haraj.com.sa/en/11181622306/Saudi_Engineer_Inspects_ReadyMade_Buildings/)). A non-engineering "condition record" should avoid presenting itself as a structural inspection. Check whether Ejar/REGA plan a native feature (risk of a government feature crowding it out). Data privacy is governed by the PDPL (not researched).

**HOME GAP 2 — Flat-fee online interior e-design for first-time homeowners**
- Western models: Havenly, Houzz, Modsy. **[UNVERIFIED, training knowledge]** Modsy shut down in 2022, which shows the pure e-design model is fragile without furniture-commerce margin. Havenly survived by pairing design with affiliate/retail commerce.
- Local problem and demand: 48k+ families moved into their first home in H1 2025 alone — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1832782). Homeownership rose from 47% to 65.4% (2016–2024) — [Amlak](https://amlak.net.sa/en/82829/).
- Competitors: furniture e-commerce (Baytonia, Homzmart, Kemitt) — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2023/05/baytonia-closes-seed-funding-round-arabic-english); [Enterprise](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2021/11/28/homzmart-launches-in-ksa-this-week/). **No funded e-design platform found** (Instagram studios not assessed).
- Revenue model: fixed-price room packages (e.g., majlis or bedroom concept plus shoppable list), affiliate commission from Baytonia/IKEA-type retailers, and optional AI renders (this overlaps with the known virtual-staging gap and could be one product).
- Difficulty/capital: **Low–moderate**. It suits a designer founder directly and needs no inventory.
- Regulatory: no specific licence found for remote decor design. Fit-out/contracting work would need commercial registration and contractor classification (not verified).

**HOME GAP 3 — Fair-rent / rent-freeze compliance and pricing tool (renters and landlords)**
- Western model: US/EU rent-control and rent-estimate tools (Zillow Rent Zestimate; Berlin Mietspiegel calculators) **[UNVERIFIED, no source fetched]**.
- Local problem: Rent increases are banned in Riyadh for 5 years. Fines reach up to 12 months' rent, and whistleblowers get a share — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2616697/amp). Tenants need to know whether a quoted rent breaches the last registered value, and landlords need to price new units legally.
- Competitors: REGA's official Rental Index — [REGA](https://rega.gov.sa/en/media-center/news-announcements/rental-index-over-294-000-transactions-recorded-in-september/). Aqar/Aamar may add pricing features. Competition: **moderate (government data) / no dedicated consumer tool found**.
- Revenue: freemium for tenants, paid landlord pricing reports, lead generation to brokers.
- Difficulty: **Low capital**, but it depends on data access (Ejar data isn't openly available as an API; not verified). There is a high risk the government or Aqar builds it. **Treat as a feature, not a company.**

**LOGISTICS GAP 1 — On-demand "few items / single bulky item" moves (Dolly, Lugg, Bellhop model), bundled with junk removal (the known LoadUp gap)**
- Western models: Dolly, Lugg, Bellhop **[UNVERIFIED, training knowledge; funding not fetched]**.
- Local problem: Moving is booked via WhatsApp/Haraj with no fixed pricing. The only apps found have negligible traction — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1435730716); [Mangoul](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1532634875). Haraj-heavy second-hand furniture trading implies frequent single-item pickups (inference).
- Demand: first-home moves (above); rent freeze may reduce tenant churn in Riyadh (a negative for moving demand there — inference).
- Competitors: Mangoul (quote marketplace), errand apps (unverified). **Weak.**
- Revenue: per-job commission (15–25% typical in Western marketplaces **[UNVERIFIED]**), plus fees for disposal or donation drop-off.
- Difficulty/capital: **Moderate**. Asset-light if it onboards existing dyna/pickup owners, but driver supply, insurance and damage claims add operational load.
- Regulatory: TGA licensing for goods-transport apps and vehicles likely applies. TGA rules restrict passenger-app driving to Saudis — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/saudia/fgup6kbzwv). Whether similar Saudization applies to goods-delivery apps is **not verified**. Municipal (Balady) rules govern bulky-waste disposal (not researched).

**LOGISTICS GAP 2 — Vetted, fixed-price moving-quote marketplace for full household moves (Western: HireAHelper, MovingHelper, UK "Compare My Move" lead-gen)**
- Western evidence: **[UNVERIFIED, no source fetched]**.
- Local problem: phone/WhatsApp quotes, no reviews, damage disputes (inference). The press-release example shows movers still quote by phone/WhatsApp — [PR](https://www.mydailyrecord.com/online_features/press_releases/10-year-riyadh-mover-offers-local-and-international-furniture-relocation/article_cb8492d0-28f0-5c25-a4fe-91dc0037b5dd.html).
- Competitors: Mangoul (unfunded; traction unknown). **Weak.**
- Revenue: lead fees from movers, or a booking commission with escrow. Upsells: packing, storage (partner with WheeKeep), cleaning, handyman (B8ak-type).
- Difficulty: **Low capital** (pure lead-gen website plus SEO in Arabic). This suits a digital founder best among the logistics ideas.
- Regulatory: Movers need a commercial registration. Platform licensing with TGA is unclear (gap).

**LOGISTICS GAP 3 — P2P storage (Neighbor) / junk removal (LoadUp)**: already known gaps. Note that the only storage competitor found is operator-run WheeKeep ($8M Series A), which validates storage demand but leaves P2P untouched — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2024/05/saudi-wheekeep-closes-8-million-series-round).

**NOT gaps (strong local competition; avoid):** rent BNPL (Rize, Ejari, Ajras), shipping aggregation for small sellers (Torod, OTO), fulfilment/last mile (Salasa, Saee), and general home maintenance booking (B8ak, Ajeer, Nabeeh, moderate).

### Inferences
- Moving marketplace, rental condition reports and e-design are the lowest-capital, best skill-fit options. All can start as a website or WhatsApp-integrated web app before building native apps.
- The most defensible combination is a "move-in bundle" sold to first-time homeowners and new tenants: condition report plus moving quote plus design package plus storage or junk-removal partners.
- Capital-heavy (flag): any rent financing, own-fleet moving/junk removal, and operator-run storage.

### Gaps
- Western funding and traction figures for Neighbor, LoadUp, Dolly, Lugg, Havenly, Rhino and InventoryBase were not verified in this session (fetches failed, budget limited). The report writer should treat Western success claims as unverified unless another researcher sourced them.
- TGA licensing requirements for goods-moving platforms were not confirmed.
- Whether Ejar has, or plans, a condition-report or deposit-holding feature is unknown. This is critical for Home Gap 1.
- Instagram-based Saudi interior design studios and Arabic-only moving apps were not systematically surveyed (app-store search in Arabic was limited to one query).
