- **Format:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 60 fps, H.264 High with AAC 48 kHz stereo; -14 LUFS / -1.2 dBTP.
- **Visuals:** every slide shown is a render of `template02/Vanta_Business_Presentation_Template_02.pptx`. There are no people or stock media, and nothing from the Business Sales Template.

## Reference analysis (`promo/Vanta_Template_02_Reel_9x16.mp4`)
Engagement principles kept:
1. Real slides in motion within the first 0.3 s.
2. Spoken and written hooks are identical, and the payload word is in the accent colour.
3. A second big contrast change around 3 s, where viewers decide whether to keep watching.
4. A reveal → read rhythm every 2–3 s, with no still stretch over about 3 s.
5. Specific numbers as proof (giant counters).
6. A list or checklist that builds, creating an open loop.
7. Pain or benefit before the product name.

Fixed from the reference: an empty first frame, and a "Link in bio" that was small and late.

Changed for retargeting: no brand introduction; each ad opens on the viewer's hesitation or the reason to buy.

- **Verbal hook:** "You've already looked at it. So here's exactly what you get."
- **Written hook:** HERE'S EXACTLY WHAT YOU GET.
- **Visual hook:** real slides fan out of the cover from frame 0.
- **Voiceover:** "Sixty finished slides, from agenda to next steps. Forty-two native charts. Edit the data, and the chart follows. Performance, KPI and campaign dashboards. Sales, finance, strategy and market analysis, already structured. Speaker notes on every slide. From a blank slide, you'd build all of that yourself."
- **Visuals and slides:**
  - the 60-slide contact sheet
  - growth chart (23), then cash flow and waterfall (41, 42), with an Edit Data chip
  - dashboards 19, 20 and 56
  - sections 30, 38, 44 and 14
  - slide 19 with its real speaker note typed into a notes pane
  - a blank slide surrounded by the deck
- **CTA:** GET IT — LINK IN BIO

## R2: professional result (dark "presentation mode")
- **Verbal hook:** "Picture this on the screen in your next meeting."
- **Written hook:** the same line.
- **Visual hook:** the cover on a glowing projected screen at frame 0, with a slide counter.
- **Voiceover:** "It opens with a clear agenda. Your results land on a real dashboard. Your numbers are explained, not just shown. Your strategy reads like a plan. And it closes with the decision you need. That's Business Presentation Pro. Link in bio."
- **Slides:** 1 → 3 → 19 → 23 → 42 → 44 → 46 → 59 → 60, advanced like a live presentation, with close-ups on details.
- **CTA:** Present like this. GET IT — LINK IN BIO

Georgia and Arial, standard on Windows and Mac. Your content? Sixty slides, from agenda to next steps. No more blank slides. Get Business Presentation Pro. Link in bio."
- **Proof shown:**

  - **Fonts:** a close-up on slide 59 with Georgia and Arial labels.
  - **Content:** a riffle through all 60 slides.
- **CTA:** NO MORE BLANK SLIDES. GET IT — LINK IN BIO
