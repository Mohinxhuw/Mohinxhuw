# Saudi Market Gaps: Automotive and Travel & Tourism (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: 19 tool calls (searches plus one failed fetch). Most findings come from search-result summaries of press and aggregator pages (Wamda, Argaam, CB Insights, Gulf News and similar). Startup funding figures are company-reported. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from vendor or blog sources, or from background knowledge with no source fetched in this session.

## Q1. What are the biggest unsolved pains for Saudi drivers and travellers (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
Drivers: the main pains are trust in used-car condition and history (accident data is spread across government portals), unclear maintenance pricing, poor parking apps, and a rigid fines-objection process. Travellers: the main pains are flight disruption with low uptake of the new GACA compensation rights, outbound visa friction (Schengen through VFS) and domestic trip planning. Domestic tourism is very large: 93.3M trips in 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Automotive**
- Accident and vehicle history is spread across channels. Accident reports are looked up by accident number through traffic services. A dealer blog claims a VIN lookup exists via SASO, and Najm (founded 2007, 27 member insurers) links accident data to ID/VIN. No consumer-facing Najm VIN report is described, and the search found no evidence that Carfax covers KSA — [Syarah guide](https://syarah.com/carsguide/check-car-accident-history/), [Syarah VIN guide](https://syarah.com/carsguide/vin-accident-check/) (dealer blog, weak evidence)
- A Saudi marketplace article names "Muajaz" as a car-history site, with no detail on what it covers — [Syarah VIN guide](https://syarah.com/carsguide/vin-accident-check/) **[UNVERIFIED]**
- Used-car valuation is done by marketplaces, not neutral bodies. Motory sells "سعّر سيارتك" in three tiers (AI quick, expert-reviewed, Pro report delivered by WhatsApp) and warns the AI can be wrong. CarSwitch KSA offers a free valuation and cites 15–20% depreciation in year 1, 10–15% a year after that, and typical mileage of 15–25k km a year — [Motory](https://ksa.motory.com/ar/%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%85/), [CarSwitch KSA](https://ksa.carswitch.com/newsroom/2026/05/20/كيف-تحسب-قيمة-سيارتك-المستعملة-قبل-الب/)
- Parking: Mawgif's consumer app has 1M+ downloads but a 1.3/5 rating from 6K+ ratings. The Riyadh Parking project (launched Aug 2024) regulates more than 140,000 residential and 24,000 paid commercial spaces across 12 zones, with permits issued through the Riyadh Parking app. Parking is run under municipal concessions (for example stc solutions with Remat Al-Riyadh) — [mwm.ai Mawgif listing](https://mwm.ai/apps/mawgif/1006045300), [Ajel](https://english.ajel.sa/economie/riyadh-parking-project-launches-managed-parking-in-residential-areas), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/mawgif-vs-smart-cities-solutions)
- Traffic fines: objections are filed online through Absher, are allowed only once per violation, and are capped at five rejected automated objections per Hijri year. The driver must still pay to avoid the fine rising to its maximum. No official acceptance rate was found — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/P63BcX/), [Almuraba](https://www.almuraba.net/شروط-الاعتراض-على-المخالفات-المرورية/)

**Travel**
- 2025 totals: about 123M visitors (+6%) and SAR304B spending (+7%). Domestic: 93.3M trips and SAR127.1B. Inbound: 29.3M visitors and SAR176.6B. Q1 2026 domestic: 28.9M tourists (+16%) and SAR34.7B — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/story/business%2Ftourism%2Fsaudi-tourism-hits-new-high-with-123-million-visitors-and-sr304b-in-spending-1.500580283), [GDN](https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1398040/Saudi-tourism-spending-reaches-record-$81bn-in-2025). An earlier preliminary release gave 122M (+5%) — [Cairoscene](https://cairoscene.com/Buzz/Saudi-Arabia-Records-122-Million-Visitors-in-2025)
- Domestic travellers averaged 18.6 nights in H1 2025, more than twice the inbound figure — [Connecting Travel](https://connectingtravel.com/news/saudi-arabia-visitor-numbers-rise-to-more-than-609-million-in-h1-2025)
- Outbound: 17.4M international trips by Saudis in 2025, which puts KSA among the top 25 outbound markets — [WTM Riyadh Trends 2026](https://www.wtm.com/portfolio/en-gb/blog/reports/wtm-spotlight-riyadh-trends-report-2026.html). One forecast says the outbound market could exceed US$60B and that spend per traveller is about 11x the global average (industry forecast, weak evidence) — [ITIJ](https://www.itij.com/latest/news/saudi-outbound-travel-market-could-exceed-us60-billion)
- Schengen: about 505,455 applications from KSA in 2024 with about a 5.9% refusal rate. This is a secondary visa-guide source; verify against official EU statistics — [HelloSafe](https://hellosafe.com/schengen-visa/from-saudi-arabia) **[UNVERIFIED]**. Applications go through VFS centres in Riyadh, Jeddah and Khobar/Dammam. The Dutch embassy tells applicants to "book directly to avoid extra costs", and the Netherlands accepts applications 6 months to 45 days before travel — [Netherlands Worldwide](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-saudi-arabia), [Denmark](https://saudiarabien.um.dk/en/travel-and-residence/practical-information/visa/where-to-apply), [Switzerland](https://www.eda.admin.ch/countries/saudi-arabia/en/home/visa/entry-ch/up-90-days/where-to-apply-schengen.html)
- Schengen travel insurance must cover at least €30,000 of medical costs plus repatriation, and must match the travel dates — [HelloSafe](https://hellosafe.com/schengen-visa/travel-insurance/from-saudi-arabia), [Giraffy](https://giraffy.com/ksa/en/learn/insurance/travel-insurance/schengen-guide) (vendor guides)
- Flight disruption: GACA's monthly complaint counts for 2025 were 1,486 (Jan), 1,169 (Feb), 1,267 (Apr), 1,370 (Jun), 2,313 (Aug), 2,399 (Sep) and 1,620 (Oct). "Flights" was the top category in several months, followed by baggage and tickets — [GACA Apr 2025](https://gaca.gov.sa/News/gaca-april-2025-complaints-report), [Riyadh Daily Aug 2025](https://alriyadhdaily.com/article/44b842fb192e4f99b2271b375a22f73a), [Argaam Oct](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1862068)
- The GACA Customer Rights regulation applies to every carrier departing KSA airports and to Saudi carriers flying into KSA. Lost, damaged or delayed baggage ranks first among compensation cases. Complaints can be filed on 8001189888 — [Aleqtsad](https://www.aleqtsad.org/?p=111926), [Al Jazeera guide 2025](https://www.aljazeera.net/travel/2025/8/24/دليلك-لطلب-التعويض-هل-تأخرت-رحلتك-أو)

### Inferences
- Complaints run at about 1,200–2,400 a month, while millions of passengers are affected by delays, so most eligible passengers probably never claim. This is inferred: no passenger-level eligibility data was found.
- The biggest driver pain that a digital product can address with little capital is the information gap on used-car history, condition and fair price, and on repair pricing. Parking and fines are controlled by government or concession holders.

### Gaps
- No survey data on Saudi driver satisfaction or used-car fraud rates was found. Odometer-rollback prevalence in KSA was not found either.
- No GACA figure for total compensation paid in 2025–2026 was found beyond the already-known SR58M.

## Q2. Which Saudi/GCC startups already serve these needs?

### Takeaway
Car marketplaces, insurance aggregation, holiday rentals and OTAs are strongly covered by well-funded players (Syarah, Tameeni, Gathern, Almosafer, Wego). Repair-price comparison, independent vehicle history reports, visa concierge and Arabic flight-claim services show weak or no local competition.

### Cited Findings
**Automotive players**
- **Syarah** (Riyadh, founded 2015). Raised $20M Series B in 2021 (Impact46 and Elm), about $60M Series C in 2024 led by Artal (including $20M debt from Partners for Growth) and $12M from Impact46 in Sept 2026. Total raised exceeds $82M. Sells 12K+ cars a year with an inspection report, 5-day money-back guarantee and 1-year warranty — [Wamda 2026](https://www.wamda.com/2026/09/saudi-autotech-syarah-raises-12-million-impact46), [Rasmal](https://www.rasmal.com/syarah-secures-60-million-in-series-c-funding/), [Cairoscene](https://cairoscene.com/Business/KSA-Car-Marketplace-Syarah-Raises-20M-to-Expand-Reach-Services). Competition: **strong** in managed used-car retail.
- **CarSwitch** (UAE) runs in KSA with a 200-point inspection. **Kayishha**, the Saudi arm of SellAnyCar, raised $35M led by Sanabil (PIF) — [adsmehub](https://www.adsmehub.ae/en/explore/post-details/carswitch-expands-to-saudi-arabia), [StartupBahrain](https://startupbahrain.com/news/used-car-marketplace-sellanycar-expands-into-saudi-arabia)
- **Motory** offers paid valuation tiers — [Motory](https://ksa.motory.com/ar/%D8%AA%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%8A%D9%85/). Funding was not found.
- **Pre-purchase inspection (Lemon Squad model). The task listed this as "not yet verified"; it is now verified to exist.** **Fahis** is an online pre-purchase inspection platform for individuals and companies across several provinces, with a SCEGA licence number listed — [Terrapinn exhibitor](https://www.terrapinn.com/exhibition/mobility-live-saudi/exhibitor-fahis.stm). **CarTrust** (Jeddah, founded 2022) does AI-assisted inspection and evaluation, is pre-seed and backed by Misk Accelerator — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ml/app/cartrust/id1581827929), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/cartrust-vs-voom). Soum is hiring a "Car Inspector" — [Lever](https://jobs.lever.co/soum/f444d10c-4df3-406c-a62d-f38d1a7b3d9d). Competition: **moderate**.
- **Repair and maintenance:** Speero (Riyadh, after-sales platform) raised $1.8M pre-Series A (Nuwa, Eq2) and claims 750K registered customers — [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/speero-raises-1-8m-for-auto-services), [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/1892736/amp). Carefer raised $2.9M pre-Series A led by Elm and runs cloud workshops — [Waya](https://waya.media/carefer-raises-2-9m-in-a-pre-series-a-round/). MisMar (Riyadh) raised $200K in Nov 2024 — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/mismar-vs-service-my-car). Garage (Khobar) raised $860K in total and CB Insights lists it as "Dead" — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/garage-3). Okhtboot (mobile car wash) raised SAR1M pre-seed — [Mubasher](https://en.sharikatmubasher.com/news/article/21427565/okhtboot-raises-sar-1-mn-pre-seed-round?lang=en). The search found **no dedicated, transparent repair-price comparison platform**.
- **Insurance:** Tameeni (Rasan) raised SAR90M (~$24M) in 2021 led by Impact46. It works with 20+ insurers and claims about 8M users and $674M of policies bought in 2021. It also sells travel insurance and is a licensed e-broker — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1512836), [Forbes ME](https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/lists/the-middle-easts-top-25-fintech-companies-2022/rasan/), [Tameeni travel](https://travel.tameeni.com/en). Competition: **strong**.
- **Parking:** Mawgif (Jeddah, B2G parking management, 1.3/5 app rating) and MAWQFI (Riyadh, find-and-reserve parking) — [Parking.net](https://www.parking.net/parking-industry/mawqfi). Funding was not found.

**Travel players**
- **Almosafer (Seera):** Q1 2025 bookings were SAR1.73B (+8% YoY), with domestic at 42% of bookings and BNPL at 25% of bookings. Its Mawasim arm covers Hajj/Umrah, and it plans an IPO in 2–3 years — [Mubasher](https://sharikatmubasher.com/news/article/21488932/almosafer-eyes-ipo-and-expansion-into-emerging-markets?lang=en). Competition: **strong**.
- **Wego** partnered with the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah on an integrated Umrah platform (flights, hotels, transport, visa requests); licensed providers deliver services on the ground — [TradeArabia](https://www.tradearabia.com/News/463959/Wego-Saudi-Ministry-launch-integrated-digital-Umrah-platform). **Nusuk Umrah** (government) launched on 20 Aug 2025 for direct visa and service booking — [Travel Trade Journal](https://traveltradejournal.com/saudi-launches-new-nusuk-umrah-platform-to-streamline-visa-and-travel-bookings-for-pilgrims/). Umrah/Hajj logistics competition: **strong and state-controlled**.
- **Gathern** raised $72M Series B in Aug 2025 led by Sanabil, at a valuation above $266M. It claims 5M users, a 44% share of alternative hospitality and 72K listings, and was the first Ministry of Tourism P2P licence holder. It is preparing a Tadawul IPO — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2025/08/gathern-raises-72-million-series-b-ahead-tadawul-ipo), [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/saudi-arabian-vacation-rental-platform-gathern-lands-72-million-dollars). Competition: **strong**.
- **Flight compensation:** AirHelp has an Arabic-language Saudi page — [AirHelp ar-sa](https://www.airhelp.com/ar-sa/flight-compensation-in-saudi-arabia/). A Saudi-domain blog, tryadel.sa ("عادل"), turned up in search with a post on Saudi passenger rights. The domain did not resolve when fetched, and nothing else about it was found. **[UNVERIFIED]: possibly a local claims or consumer-rights entrant; it should be checked manually before saying "no local service exists".**
- **Visa concierge:** no Saudi or GCC visa-concierge startup appeared in searches. Official embassy pages send applicants to VFS directly — [Netherlands Worldwide](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-saudi-arabia).

### Inferences
- The well-funded categories (marketplaces, insurance aggregation, rentals, OTAs, Umrah) are closed to a low-capital solo founder.
- Pre-purchase inspection is no longer an empty gap. It could still work as a niche, for example a buyer's-agent service for private Haraj deals, but it needs inspectors in the field.

### Gaps
- Funding was not found for Motory, Fahis, Mawgif or MAWQFI. Rehlat's position was not found.
- Offline travel agencies that offer visa services (common in KSA, from background knowledge) were not researched, and their digital maturity is unknown.

## Q3. Which Western models are proven but have no strong Saudi equivalent? (Gap cards)

### Takeaway
Best fits for a low-capital, design-led founder: (A) an Arabic flight-compensation and passenger-rights claim service, (B) a visa-concierge and document-prep service for outbound Saudis (Atlys/VisaHQ model), and (C) transparent repair-price quotes and comparison (RepairPal/WhoCanFixMyCar model). A Carfax-style vehicle history report is the largest gap, but it depends on access to government data and needs capital or partnerships.

### Cited Findings and Gap Cards

**Gap T1: Arabic flight-delay and baggage compensation claims (AirHelp model).** Competition: **weak**. Foreign players only, plus the unverified tryadel.sa.
- Local problem and demand: GACA rules apply to all departures from KSA. GACA logged 1,100–2,400 complaints a month in 2025, and flights and baggage are the top categories — [GACA](https://gaca.gov.sa/News/gaca-april-2025-complaints-report), [Aleqtsad](https://www.aleqtsad.org/?p=111926). SR58M paid in compensation (already known to the caller).
- Western model evidence: AirHelp runs an Arabic KSA page — [AirHelp](https://www.airhelp.com/ar-sa/flight-compensation-in-saudi-arabia/). AirHelp's revenue, fee level (about 35% contingency in the EU) and claimed millions of customers are **[UNVERIFIED]: background knowledge, not fetched this session.**
- Revenue model: contingency fee (no win, no fee), an optional paid "fast-track", and B2B white-label for travel agents.
- Difficulty and capital: low. A web form, document handling and an Arabic claim-letter workflow; GACA complaint escalation adds a manual step.
- Regulatory: representing claimants for a fee may touch Saudi rules on legal representation and agency, which need a lawyer's check **[UNVERIFIED]**. A free official channel exists (GACA 8001189888), so the product's value is convenience and success rate.

**Gap T2: Visa concierge and document prep for Saudi outbound travellers (Atlys / VisaHQ / iVisa model).** Competition: **none found** for a digital-first local player.
- Western model evidence: Atlys raised a $36M Series C in March 2026 (Susquehanna Asia VC, with MakeMyTrip), a $20M Series B in 2024, $12M Series A and $4.25M seed. It reports about 450K visas processed since the Series B and a 700K+ annual run rate across 120+ destinations, with the UAE among its markets — [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/atlys-raises-usd-36-mn-in-series-c-round/503468), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/visa-processing-platform-atlys-raises-20-mn-to-boost-product-stack-expand-market-footprint/). (Atlys is Indian/US, which is a proven pattern though not strictly Western.)
- Local demand: 17.4M outbound trips in 2025 — [WTM](https://www.wtm.com/portfolio/en-gb/blog/reports/wtm-spotlight-riyadh-trends-report-2026.html) — and about 505K Schengen applications in 2024 — [HelloSafe](https://hellosafe.com/schengen-visa/from-saudi-arabia) **[UNVERIFIED]**. The process involves VFS appointments, document lists and insurance at €30K minimum — [Netherlands Worldwide](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/schengen-visa/apply-saudi-arabia).
- Revenue model: service fee per application of about SAR100–300 **[assumption]**, plus affiliate commission on Schengen-compliant travel insurance (Tameeni's travel product is the e-broker incumbent — [Tameeni](https://travel.tameeni.com/en)) and on flights and hotels.
- Difficulty and capital: low to moderate. The core is a checklist and form-prep SaaS plus human review. Saudis already get visa-free or e-visa access to many destinations, so the target is Schengen, UK, US and Asia.
- Regulatory: embassies warn against intermediaries and appointment resale ("book directly to avoid extra costs"). Do not resell VFS slots. Selling insurance requires being an Insurance Authority-licensed broker or an affiliate of one.

**Gap A1: Transparent repair and maintenance quotes and price comparison (RepairPal / WhoCanFixMyCar / Openbay model).** Competition: **weak**. No dedicated price-comparison platform was found; adjacent players are Speero, Carefer, MisMar and on-demand mechanics.
- Local evidence: existing startups are vertically integrated (workshops or mobile service), not neutral price-transparency marketplaces — [Waya/Carefer](https://waya.media/carefer-raises-2-9m-in-a-pre-series-a-round/), [Techleap/Speero](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/speero-raises-1-8m-for-auto-services). Garage's failure ("Dead" on CB Insights) shows that the on-demand operations model is hard — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/garage-3).
- Western model evidence: RepairPal's fair-price estimator and certified-shop network and WhoCanFixMyCar's UK quote marketplace **[UNVERIFIED]: background knowledge, not fetched this session.**
- Revenue model: workshop subscription or lead fees, booking commission, and a fair-price report sold to consumers or fleets.
- Difficulty and capital: low to moderate. The hard part is the supply side (onboarding workshops). It can start as a crowdsourced, receipt-based price index ("كم دفعت؟") in one city.
- Regulatory: commercial registration and an e-commerce licence. Low risk.

**Gap A2: Independent vehicle history report (Carfax / AutoCheck / UK HPI model).** Competition: **weak** (Muajaz [UNVERIFIED], fragmented government lookups, marketplace inspections). **CAPITAL- AND PARTNERSHIP-HEAVY.**
- Local evidence: history data sits with Najm (accidents), traffic/Absher (fines, transfers) and possibly SASO (VIN), and consumers navigate these separately — [Syarah guide](https://syarah.com/carsguide/vin-accident-check/). Elm, the government tech company, already invests in Syarah and Carefer — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2026/09/saudi-autotech-syarah-raises-12-million-impact46), [Waya](https://waya.media/carefer-raises-2-9m-in-a-pre-series-a-round/). That suggests the data owner would be a partner or a competitor (inference).
- Regulatory: requires PDPL-compliant data-sharing agreements with Najm, Elm or Absher. Hard for a solo founder. Possible low-capital wedge: a buyer checklist and report aggregator that guides users through the official lookups plus an import-history check for US-imported cars **[assumption]**.

**Not recommended (weak fit):**
- Fines-contest service (DoNotPay model): Absher objection is free and limited to one per violation — [Almuraba](https://www.almuraba.net/شروط-الاعتراض-على-المخالفات-المرورية/).
- Parking: controlled by municipal concessions — [Ajel](https://english.ajel.sa/economie/riyadh-parking-project-launches-managed-parking-in-residential-areas).
- Umrah logistics: state-controlled through Nusuk and licensed providers — [Travel Trade Journal](https://traveltradejournal.com/saudi-launches-new-nusuk-umrah-platform-to-streamline-visa-and-travel-bookings-for-pilgrims/).
- Car insurance comparison: Tameeni and others are strong — [Argaam](https://www.argaam.com/en/article/articledetail/id/1512836).

### Inferences
- Domestic trip planning (Wanderlog/TripIt model) is a possible fourth travel gap. Domestic trips are very long (18.6 nights), Almosafer gets 42% of bookings from domestic travel, and no Saudi AI itinerary product appeared in searches. Gathern's AI plans are vague. It is hard to monetize on its own, so it is better as an affiliate content and product layer. Competition looks weak, but this was not verified.
- T1 and T2 share a customer (the outbound or flying Saudi) and could be one brand: an Arabic "travel rights and documents" assistant.

### Gaps
- Western comparator metrics (AirHelp revenue, RepairPal, Carfax, Lemon Squad) were not fetched this session and need sourcing.
- The legality of paid claim representation and visa-assistance businesses under Saudi law was not verified; a lawyer or MISA/MoC check is needed.
- The identity of tryadel.sa was not established.
- App-store searches (Arabic keywords) for visa or flight-claim apps were not run directly.
